## Idea

A messenger without a phone number or a central server. Your identity is a key on your device, and you add people by QR code, link or by meeting nearby.

## How it works

One message, two paths: over Bluetooth nearby, without the internet, or through Nostr relays at any distance. Messages are encrypted on the device and only the recipient can read them.

## Where it runs

iOS: version 1.1. macOS, Windows, Linux and browser: preview 0.1.8. CLI for macOS and Windows: preview. Android and CLI for Linux are in development.
